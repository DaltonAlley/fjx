# Community code of conduct

Everyone participating in fjx issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project
spaces is expected to:

- Be respectful and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels
  of experience.
- Discuss the work, not the worth of the person doing it.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect people's privacy, time, and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized abuse, deliberate disruption,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a public moderation concern, ask the repository maintainer to review the
relevant discussion. Do not repeat abusive content or publish private details.
If a report needs privacy, ask for a private contact channel without describing
the incident publicly. GitHub's **Report abuse** tools are also available for
conduct that violates GitHub's rules, including concerns about a maintainer.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock a discussion, warn a participant,
or restrict participation in proportion to the behavior. Reports should be
handled discreetly and with attention to the safety of those affected.

This is a project-specific policy, not a claim of an external certification or
an around-the-clock moderation service.
